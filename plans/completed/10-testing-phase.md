## Batch 1 — Triggers and the chain: where abilities are queued, finalized and resolved; cleanup between chain items; the fuzz findings

- [x] **F1** Viktor, Innovator 117 triggers on its own Recruit token and the chain never empties (686 of 100,000 fuzz games); and `ChainManager::processFEPR` gives up after 1,000 passes, drops the chain with a warning and plays on instead of failing the game — CERTAIN (fuzz) — FIXED: Viktor fires on `WhenYouPlayACard` / `WhenYouPlayMe` with the token and "on an opponent's turn" tests in `triggerCondition` (CR 350.2, 383.2.a.1); `ChainManager::processFEPR`'s bound throws `std::logic_error` naming the newest item (`abandonChain` is gone), except a loop of mandatory actions, which is a draw (see F8). The other quiet bounds throw as well (Main Phase 500 actions, showdown 100, priority window, 20 Cleanups, Expiration Step 8 passes); a play offered at chain priority that puts nothing on the chain and an intent chain priority or Focus cannot execute throw; a concession ends the game at any decision; test: Batch1ChainFlow.F1_ViktorDoesNotTriggerOnAToken_AndTriggersOncePerCard, ChainManagerFixTest.SafetyCap_ChainThatNeverEmptiesIsAnError, SweepTriggersTest.PriorityWindow_APlayThatPutsNothingOnTheChainIsAnError
- [x] **F2** Alpha Strike 751 leaves its finalize pass before the marker when it is played by an effect or its choices are made again after a control change (21 fuzz games; engine exception) — CERTAIN (fuzz) — FIXED: `GameEngine::chooseFirstExecution` asks for the one required target of a spell that takes it from the play intent and has none (played by an effect, or making new choices) and then runs its finalize pass; Alpha Strike reads it with `chosenTarget` and says "nothing" through `splitDamageAmong`. No other finalize-pass spell trips: test: Batch1ChainFlow.F2_AlphaStrikePlayedByAnEffectChoosesItsUnitInTheFinalizePass, SweepCostsTest.CardContract_NoFinalizePassSpellTripsWhenPlayedByAnEffectOrChoosingAgain (34 spells, 136 effect plays, 100 re-choices)
- [x] **D1** Every permanent with a play trigger; Accelerate units: "when you play" abilities are queued and finalized before the permanent has entered, and Accelerate is asked after them — CERTAIN — FIXED: `CardPlayedEvent` (and "played not from hand") is emitted by `resolvePermanent` once the permanent is on the board (CR 383.4.a.2), for hand and facedown plays; [Accelerate] and the card's optional additional cost are decided before anything is paid and only if the whole is payable (CR 355.1.a); test: Batch1ChainFlow.D1_AccelerateIsChosenBeforeTheCost_AndThePlayAbilityAfterTheUnitEntered, Batch1ChainFlow.D1_AccelerateIsNotOfferedWhenItCannotBePaidOnTopOfTheCost, Batch1ChainFlow.D1_TheOptionalAdditionalCostIsChosenBeforeTheCardIsPaidFor
