## Zone Layout Summary

The playmat has 3 horizontal bands, read top to bottom:

### Top Band — Battlefield Zone + Legend/Champion (rows 6-8)
- **Battlefield Zone** (left, wide): Shared battlefields where combat happens.
  Units move here from Base to contest/control. Each BF can hold units from
  multiple players. In 1v1, 2 battlefields (one per player); can grow via tokens.
- **Legend Zone** (top right): The player's Champion Legend. Stays here all game.
- **Champion Zone** (far right): The Chosen Champion starts here, can be played
  from here like from hand.

### Middle Band — Base: Units + Gears (rows 3-5)
- **Base** (left, wide): Where permanents (units and gear) reside when not at
  a battlefield. Units enter here by default (exhausted). Gear always stays
  here unless attached to a unit that moves.
- **Main Deck** (right): Face-down draw pile. Secret information.

### Bottom Band — Base: Runes + Trash (rows 0-2)
- **Rune Deck** (far left): Face-down rune pile. 12 runes, channeled 2/turn.
- **Base: Runes** (center): Channeled runes sit here. They can be exhausted
  for Energy or recycled for Power.
- **Trash** (far right): Discard pile. Face-up, public information, unordered.

### Off-Mat
- **Hand**: Cards held privately by the player. Not on the playmat.
- **Banishment**: Cards removed from play. Tracked off to the side.
- **Chain**: Logical zone — spells/abilities being resolved. No physical space.

## Score Tracker
Left edge, numbered 0-8 (Victory Score for 1v1). Players track their score here.
